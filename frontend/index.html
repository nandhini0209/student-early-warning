    How Early Alert & Retention Operates in Our College

    A multi-stage proactive intervention model designed to prevent exam debarment and academic dropout.

    1

    Faculty Telemetry Sync

    Course teachers enter weekly lecture attendance and continuous internal marks (CIE).

    2

    AI Risk Scoring Engine

    Composite weighted formula (Attendance 40% + Marks 35% + Assignments 15% + Consecutive Absences 10%).

    3

    Automated Mentor Alert

    If thresholds breach (<75% attendance or <40% marks), the assigned faculty mentor receives an instant alert.

    4

    Guided Recovery

    Mentor conducts 1-on-1 counseling, assigns remedial support, and documents recovery before semester exams.
